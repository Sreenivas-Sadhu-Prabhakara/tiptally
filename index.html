The four methods

Pick the split that fits your shift

Weighted role-hours

weight × hours ÷ total × pool

The default. A server-hour and a busser-hour can carry different value. Fair when roles do genuinely different work.

By hours

hours ÷ total hours × pool

Simplest transparent method. Every hour is worth the same, whatever the role.

Equal split

pool ÷ number of people

Everyone takes home the same. Good for small, tight teams that share everything.

By sales (commission)

sales ÷ total sales × pool

Split proportionally to what each person rang up. Enter sales per person in the team table.

Common questions

Tip splitting, answered

How do you split tips fairly?

There is no single legal answer, but the common fair methods weight each person by how much and what they contributed. tiptally supports four: weighted role-hours (a server-hour can count more than a busser-hour, by an editable weight), by hours only, an equal split, and by individual sales for commission-style pools. Whichever you pick, the tool shows the exact formula behind every share so the team can see it is even-handed.

What is a tip-out?

A tip-out is a share of tips that front-of-house staff pass to support roles — often the bar and bussers — usually as a small percentage taken off the top before the rest of the pool is divided. For example, servers might tip out 3% to the bar and 2% to bussers. tiptally removes those amounts first, then splits what remains.

What are role weights and why change them?

A role weight scales how much a role earns per hour worked. Defaults are server 1.0, bartender 1.0, busser 0.7, host 0.6, kitchen 0.5, but every workplace is different, so all of them are editable. Set kitchen to 0 to exclude them, or raise a role to reflect your house policy.

How do the cents add up exactly?

Percentage shares almost never land on whole cents. tiptally rounds everyone down first, then hands out the leftover cents one at a time to the people with the largest fractional remainders. That is the largest-remainder method, and it guarantees the shares plus tip-outs sum to the pool to the exact cent. The sheet shows this reconciliation.

Can I split tips by hours worked?

Yes. Choose the "By hours" method and each person receives a share proportional to the hours they worked that shift, ignoring role. It is the simplest transparent method when everyone does similar work.

Does my data leave my device?

No. tiptally runs entirely in your browser. Your roster and settings are saved only in this browser via localStorage, and a strict content-security policy blocks any outbound network connection. Nothing is uploaded.
